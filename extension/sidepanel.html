New Day claim helper

Prototype · fills one claim at a time, then the next · you check and submit

1

Load claims

In the dashboard's Billing page, click "Copy all for helper" for a client (or "Copy for helper" on one claim), then paste it here.

2

Fill the portal

  1. On Patient: click Fill, check the details, click Search.
  2. Answer the other-coverage question and continue.
  3. On Enter claim: click Fill again.
  4. Check, click Predetermine, then Process claim in the portal.
  5. Click Mark submitted here. If the client has another claim, it loads next: start a new claim in the portal and repeat.
