New Day claim helper

Prototype · fills one claim at a time · you check and submit

1. Load a claim

In the dashboard's Billing page, click "Copy for helper" on a claim, then paste it here.

2. Fill the portal

  1. On Patient: click Fill, enter date of birth, click Search.
  2. Answer the other-coverage question and continue.
  3. On Enter details: click Fill again.
  4. Check, click Predetermine, then Process claim in the portal.
  5. Click Mark submitted here.
